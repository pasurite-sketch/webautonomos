If you are a dentist or physiotherapist working as a freelancer in Spain's Comunidad Valenciana, you have probably wondered whether you really need a website for your dental clinic or physiotherapy practice. The short answer: yes, and more than ever. In 2026, 77% of patients search for health professionals online before booking an appointment. If you have no digital presence, those patients choose your competitors in Valencia, Alicante or Elche.

Why a website is essential for health professionals

In the healthcare sector, trust is everything. And in 2026, the first impression is no longer made in the consulting room but on Google. According to a Doctoralia study, 82% of patients check the internet before choosing a healthcare professional. They look at reviews, clinic photos, available treatments, and above all, whether the professional inspires confidence.

Must-have sections for your dental or physiotherapy website

Not all healthcare websites are the same, but some sections are non-negotiable: a homepage that hooks visitors in 3 seconds, a complete list of treatments or services, information about the professional and the team, a location section with map and visible contact details, and patient testimonials. 90% of patients read reviews before choosing a health professional.

Healthcare web design: how to build trust from the first click

Web design for health professionals has its own rules. Blues, soft greens and whites convey cleanliness, calm and professionalism. Use readable fonts, simple navigation with a maximum of 5-6 options, and make sure your website loads in under 3 seconds and is fully responsive. Over 70% of health-related searches are made from mobile devices.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a website for a dental clinic cost?
Prices vary a great deal by model. A bespoke website can cost between €1,500 and €5,000 in one payment. With subscription models such as the one from WebAutonomos, though, you can have a professional website for your dental clinic from €15/month, with no upfront investment and no hidden costs. It includes design, hosting, SSL and maintenance.
What sections should a physiotherapist website have?
A physiotherapy website should include at least: a home page with a clear value proposition, a list of treatments and specialities, information about the practitioner and their qualifications, a location with a map, a contact or booking system, and patient testimonials. Optionally, a blog with health advice improves your ranking.
Is a website compulsory if I am a self-employed dentist or physiotherapist?
It is not a legal requirement, but in 2026 it is practically indispensable. 77% of patients look for information online before choosing a healthcare professional. Without a website, those patients go straight to your competitors. A professional site also conveys a level of trust that a social media profile cannot match.
Do I need an online booking system on my website?
It is not essential, but it is well worth having. Patients value being able to book at any hour without having to phone during consulting times. If you do not want a complex system, a simple contact form with a preferred-date field, or a link to WhatsApp Business, already makes the process far easier.
What legal requirements must a dental clinic website meet?
A dental clinic website must comply with GDPR (a privacy and cookie policy) and, in Spain, with the LSSI (a legal notice carrying the owners details). If it collects health data, it must give specific information about the processing of sensitive data. It is also advisable to display the clinics health registration number and the practitioners professional registration details.
