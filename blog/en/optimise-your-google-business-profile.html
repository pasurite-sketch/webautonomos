10 tricks to optimize your Google Business Profile listing

  6. Frequently asked questions

Your Google Business Profile listing (formerly Google My Business) is, along with your website, the most powerful tool for attracting local customers. But just having a listing is not enough: 56% of local businesses haven't even completed all their information. If you want to optimize your Google Business Profile to stand out from your competition in Valencia, Alicante, Elda or Elche, here are the 10 tricks that local marketing professionals use and that you can apply today.

Why optimizing your Google Business Profile changes everything

Fully optimized listings receive 7 times more clicks than incomplete ones. Listings with photos receive 42% more requests for directions on Google Maps and 35% more clicks to the website. Google's Local Pack (the 3 map listings) captures 44% of all clicks in local searches. Being there is the number one goal of any Google My Business optimization strategy.

Tricks 1-4: Basic information that makes the difference

Choose the perfect primary category (Google has over 4,000 categories), write a description with local keywords in the 750 characters available, fill in absolutely every information field (hours, phone, website, services, attributes) and add your services with detailed descriptions and estimated prices to build transparency.

Tricks 5-7: Photos, reviews and weekly posts

Listings with over 100 photos receive 520% more calls. Upload cover photos, logo, completed work, team photos and premises. Get more reviews and respond to all of them mentioning the service and location. Post weekly updates with photos of recent work and a call to action. Google values regular activity.

Tricks 8-10: Advanced GBP features that almost nobody uses

Enable direct messaging so customers can write to you from your listing. Post and answer frequently asked questions in the Q&A section yourself, anticipating customer queries. And connect your website to your listing maintaining NAP consistency (Name, Address, Phone) so Google understands your business is legitimate and local.

Weekly action plan to keep your listing optimized

Monday: post an update with a photo (10 min). Wednesday: review and respond to reviews (5 min). Friday: upload 2-3 new photos and check messages (10 min). Monthly: review statistics for calls, clicks and direction requests (15 min). Laura, a hairdresser in Valencia, went from 4 to 28 monthly calls in 6 months by following this plan.

Optimizing your Google Business Profile is not a one-time task. It's an ongoing but simple effort: 30 minutes per week can transform your local business visibility. Combine your GBP listing with a professional website and WhatsApp Business for an unbeatable strategy.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to see the effect of optimising my GBP listing?
Changes to Google Business Profile usually take between three and seven days to appear in full. The real impact on your ranking, though, shows four to eight weeks after applying the optimisations consistently. Consistency is the key: posting weekly and replying to reviews regularly.
Can I have a Google Business Profile listing without premises?
Yes. Google allows listings for service area businesses (SABs) that do not receive customers at a physical address. Electricians, plumbers, painters or locksmiths who travel to the customer can create a listing stating the areas they cover instead of showing an address. The address will not be publicly visible.
Is optimising Google Business Profile free?
Yes, creating and optimising your Google Business Profile listing is entirely free. You pay Google nothing. What you do need is the time to complete all the information, upload photos, reply to reviews and publish weekly updates. If you would rather a professional handled it, management services start at €29/month.
How many photos should I upload to my Google listing?
Google recommends a minimum of 10 photos for a well-optimised listing. Listings with more than 100 photos receive 520% more calls than those with none. Upload photos of your team, of completed jobs, of your premises if you have any, and of your products or services. Add at least two or three new ones each month to keep the listing active.
What do I do if someone leaves a fake negative review?
First, reply professionally and politely, setting out your version of events. Second, from your Google Business Profile dashboard you can report the review as inappropriate if you believe it is fake or spam. Google will look at the request within five to ten days. In the meantime, the best defence is having plenty of genuine positive reviews to outweigh the negative one.
