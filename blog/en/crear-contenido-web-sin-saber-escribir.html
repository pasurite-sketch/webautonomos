Why most freelancers do not write

80% of freelancers give up the idea of updating their website out of fear of the blank page. And it is understandable: writing consumes time and mental energy you do not have after a day of physical or technical work. But the problem is not your lack of literary talent: it is that you are trying to write like a professional when you should write like you speak. A website without content is a dead digital business card. And nobody hires a ghost.

Trick No. 1: speak, do not write

The fastest way to create authentic content is to dictate it out loud. Use your phone's dictation function (Whisper, iPhone Notes, or Google Voice) and record 5 minutes answering questions like: what I do, for whom, what problem I solve. The transcription will have errors, but the tone will be 100% natural. Then you correct it calmly. This method produces content that connects because it sounds authentic, not robotic. Bonus: it saves 70% of the time.

The 4 essential sections any freelancer can write

A freelancer website does not need to be long. It only needs 4 well-made sections: a Home page with a clear sentence about what you do and for whom; Services with brief descriptions and indicative prices when possible; About me with your story, your training and your values in 3 paragraphs; Contact with a simple form, phone, WhatsApp and address if applicable. Any freelancer can fill these 4 sections in an afternoon with the dictation method.

Universal template: the before/after method

For each service you offer, use this simple 3-paragraph structure: describe the problem your client has (the before); describe the result they will get with you (the after); briefly explain how you work to achieve it (the how). This formula works because it talks about the client, not about you. And that is exactly what the client wants to read when they land on your website.

How to use AI as an assistant without it showing

ChatGPT and similar tools are useful if you use them well. The golden rule: never write your content from scratch with AI. Write it yourself first (with the dictation method) and use AI only to correct spelling and grammar, simplify long sentences, generate variations of the same paragraph. If you ask AI to write everything, the result will be generic and it will show. If you use it as a proofreader, you keep your authentic voice with professional quality.

The mistake that kills most websites: generic text

The forbidden phrase on any professional website: We are a company committed to quality and customer satisfaction. This phrase says absolutely nothing and thousands of identical websites use it. Instead, be specific: I am a plumber in Elche with 12 years of experience, specialised in emergency repairs in under 2 hours. Specificity builds trust. Generalities build distrust.

Action plan: 1 hour of content per week

Do not try to write your whole website in one weekend: you will freeze. Instead, block 1 hour a week (Monday morning works well for many) and dedicate that hour to: 30 minutes dictating new content or improving existing content, 20 minutes correcting the transcription, 10 minutes publishing it on your website. In 4 weeks you will have a complete website. In 6 months you will have a blog with 24 articles that will give you SEO visibility for years.

How much content does a freelancer website really need?
Much less than you think. 4 well-made pages (Home, Services, About me, Contact) are enough for an effective professional website.
Can I use ChatGPT to write all my content?
It is not recommended. Use AI as a proofreader and assistant, not as a writer. Content generated 100% by AI sounds generic and reduces trust.
