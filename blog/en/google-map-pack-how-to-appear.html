What is the Google Map Pack and how to get your business listed

If you have ever searched for "plumber in Valencia" or "hairdresser Alicante" on Google, you will have seen a block with a map and three highlighted businesses right above all the results. That is the Google Map Pack, and it captures between 60% and 75% of all clicks on local searches. For a freelancer in Spain's Comunidad Valenciana, appearing there means more calls, more visits, and more customers. In this guide we explain exactly what the Google local pack is, how its algorithm works, and the concrete steps to get your business into one of those three privileged positions.

What is the Google Map Pack and why it matters so much

The Google Map Pack (also called the Local Pack) is the block of local results that appears at the top of Google when the search engine detects a local search intent. According to a Moz study, the Map Pack receives between 60% and 75% of all clicks on searches with local intent. Additionally, 76% of people who perform a local search on mobile visit a business or call within the next 24 hours.

The 3 factors that determine your position in the Map Pack

Google uses three main factors: Relevance (how well your listing matches the user's search), Distance (proximity of your business to the user), and Prominence (your "digital reputation" based on reviews, website, directories, and listing activity). Of these three, relevance and prominence are 100% within your control, and that is where you should focus your efforts.

How to optimize your listing to appear in the Map Pack

Your Google Business Profile is your ticket into the Map Pack. Choose the correct primary category, complete 100% of your listing, write an optimized description with your service and area, upload photos weekly (listings with more than 100 photos receive 520% more calls), and publish Google Posts weekly to signal activity.

The role of your website and reviews in the Map Pack

Google cross-references your listing information with your website. Businesses with their own website optimized for local SEO are up to 50% more likely to appear in the Map Pack. Reviews are also decisive: quality matters more than quantity. Actively ask for reviews, respond to all of them, and encourage customers to mention the type of service and the city.

Complete strategy to dominate the Map Pack in your area

Month 1: create or claim your listing, complete it 100%, upload photos, and build your optimized website. Month 2: request reviews, publish weekly posts, and register in local directories. Month 3: create local content on your website and analyze statistics. Month 4 onwards: maintain consistency. Lucia, a beautician in Alicante, followed this strategy and went from 3 to 18 monthly calls from Google in just 3 months.

The Map Pack is the most important source of free calls for local businesses. Appearing in those three privileged positions requires consistently working on your Google listing, your website, and your reviews. There are no shortcuts, but the results are cumulative: each month your business gains more authority and it becomes harder for a competitor to displace you.

Frequently asked questions

What exactly is the Google Map Pack?
The Map Pack (also called the Local Pack) is the block of three results with a map that Google shows when it detects a search with local intent. Search for plumber Valencia, for instance, and you will see a map and three featured businesses above the organic results. Those three places receive between 60% and 75% of all clicks on local searches.
How long does it take for my business to appear in the Map Pack?
It depends on the competition in your area and sector. For low-competition searches — locksmith in Elda, say — you can appear in the Map Pack within 4 to 8 weeks with a well-optimised listing. For more competitive searches such as dentist Valencia, it can take three to six months of steady work on your listing, your reviews and your website. Consistency is the key.
Do I need a website to appear in the Map Pack?
Technically it is not compulsory, but having your own website is one of the heaviest factors in Map Pack ranking. Google cross-checks the information on your listing against your site to confirm that your business is real and relevant. Businesses with their own website are up to 50% more likely to appear in the top three Map Pack places than those without one.
Can I pay to appear in the Map Pack?
You cannot pay to appear in the organic Map Pack. Google does offer Local Service Ads, which appear above the Map Pack in some sectors. For the organic Map Pack, position is determined by how well your listing is optimised, your reviews, your proximity to the user and the signals from your website. It is entirely merit, not money.
What is the difference between the Map Pack and the organic results?
The Map Pack shows Google Business Profile listings — name, address, phone, reviews and a map — while the organic results show web pages. The Map Pack appears above the organic results and receives far more clicks on local searches. Ideally you work on both: your Google listing for the Map Pack and your website for the organic results. That way you cover both routes.
