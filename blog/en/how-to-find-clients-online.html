How to Get Clients Online as a Freelancer

If you're a freelancer in the Valencian Community and want to get clients online, this guide is for you. Whether you're an electrician in Elda, a plumber in Elche, a dentist in Valencia or a carpenter in Alicante: in 2026, 87% of Spaniards search for professional services on Google before calling anyone.

The problem isn't that there aren't enough clients, but that those clients can't find you. Here we give you the concrete, proven and affordable strategies to change that.

Why the internet is the best channel to get clients in 2026

According to the INE, in 2025, 94% of Spanish households have internet connection, and 87% of consumers search for information about local services on Google before making a decision. The pattern is always the same: the client searches, compares options and calls the one they trust the most.

Your website: the foundation for attracting clients online

If you had to choose just one tool to get clients online, it would be your own website. Your website is the only space on the internet you control 100%. It needs to be clear, fast and focused on a single action: getting the visitor to contact you.

A freelancer's website needs: clear headline, detailed services, real photos, visible phone and WhatsApp, customer reviews and Google map. From 15 euros per month, no contract.

Google Maps and reviews: your best salespeople

Google's Map Pack receives between 60% and 75% of all clicks in local searches. To stand out you need: correct category, updated photos, frequent reviews, respond to all reviews and publish weekly posts.

Local SEO: getting Google to show you first in your area

76% of people who do a local search on mobile visit a business within 24 hours. The 4 pillars: NAP consistency, localized content, directory citations and links from local websites.

WhatsApp, social media and email: complementary channels

WhatsApp Business is the number one channel: 98% of Spaniards use WhatsApp. For social media, be strategic: if your business is visual, Instagram works. If it's emergency services, your time is better spent on Google Maps and your website.

Don't try to implement everything at once. The most efficient order is: 1) Google Maps listing, 2) Professional website with local SEO, 3) WhatsApp Business, 4) Systematic reviews, 5) Social media only if your business is visual.

Frequently asked questions

How can I find clients online without spending much money?
The three most cost-effective strategies are: creating a Google Business Profile listing (free), having a professional website (from €15/month) and asking satisfied customers for reviews. With those three actions, many freelancers in the Valencian Community get between five and fifteen new calls a month.
How long does digital marketing take to work for a freelancer?
Google Maps results usually show within two to four weeks of optimising your listing. Local SEO on your website takes between two and six months to reach the top results. WhatsApp Business and reviews produce results from day one.
Which matters more, having a website or being on social media?
For a freelancer providing local services, your own website matters far more than social media. Your site appears when someone actively searches for your service on Google, whereas social media depends on the user spotting your post among hundreds of others. The ideal combination is website + Google Maps + WhatsApp Business.
Does a freelancer need to invest in Google Ads?
Not necessarily. Before spending on advertising, make sure the free channels are properly optimised: your Google listing, a website with local SEO and customer reviews. Many freelancers get enough customers from these organic channels alone. Google Ads can be useful later, to speed up results in highly competitive areas.
Which free tools can I use to attract customers online?
The best free tools for freelancers are: Google Business Profile (your Google Maps listing), WhatsApp Business (professional customer service), Canva (image design), Google Analytics (web analytics) and Google Search Console (ranking tracking). All are free and easy to use.
