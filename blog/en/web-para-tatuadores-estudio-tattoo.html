Websites for tattoo artists and tattoo studios

If you are a tattoo artist, your portfolio is everything. Instagram is great for showing your work, but it does not belong to you: one algorithm change and your visibility drops 80% overnight. Your own website is your permanent gallery, your booking system and your professional calling card. In this guide we explain what a tattoo studio website needs to attract premium clients and fill your schedule.

Why a tattoo artist needs a website if they already have Instagram

Instagram is a shop window, not a foundation. You cannot control the algorithm, you do not appear on Google when someone searches 'tattoo artist in Valencia', and you cannot process bookings or payments directly. A professional website complements Instagram: it attracts clients from Google, shows your full portfolio with no format limits, and lets you manage appointments and deposits professionally.

Essential sections for a tattoo website

A visual portfolio with high-resolution images organised by style (realism, traditional, blackwork, watercolour), information about the artist and the studio, a step-by-step work process (from consultation to aftercare), a booking system with a deposit option, the studio location with photos of the interior, and a hygiene and sterilisation policy.

Web design for tattoo artists: aesthetics matter twice as much

A tattoo artist's website should reflect their artistic style. Dark or black backgrounds work very well to highlight tattoo images. The typography should be striking but legible. Avoid generic templates that look like a dentist's website. Your website should convey the same personality as your work: if you tattoo Japanese style, the website should breathe that aesthetic.

Online portfolio: beyond Instagram

Your website lets you organise your portfolio in ways Instagram cannot: categories by style, before-and-after galleries, zoom on details, and even videos of the tattooing process. Add a short description of each piece (inspiration, time, technique) to connect emotionally with visitors and improve your SEO.

Online booking and deposits

Studios that manage online bookings with a mandatory deposit have up to 60% fewer cancellations. Integrate a booking system into your website where the client chooses a date, describes their idea and pays a deposit. This filters serious clients from the curious and secures their commitment.

SEO for tattoo artists: appear on Google

Optimise your website for searches like 'realism tattoo artist Valencia', 'tattoo studio Alicante' or 'Japanese tattoos Elche'. Create a Google Business Profile listing with photos of your work and the studio. Ask satisfied clients for reviews. Local SEO is the most stable source of clients in the long term.

Your art deserves a showcase to match. A professional website is not an expense, it is an investment that attracts premium clients willing to pay for quality. And with a website well positioned on Google, you stop depending exclusively on the Instagram algorithm.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a website if I already have Instagram with 10,000 followers?
Yes. Instagram could change its algorithm tomorrow. Your website is yours forever and ranks you on Google, where the clients who are not on social media search.

What photos should I use on my website?
Professional photos of your best work, in high resolution, with good lighting. Also photos of the studio to convey cleanliness and professionalism.

With local SEO: optimise your website for your city and style, create your Google Business listing and get reviews. Most tattoo artists have no website, so you have an advantage.
