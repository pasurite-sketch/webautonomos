The 10 digital marketing mistakes freelancers make (and how to avoid them)

📑 Article contents

  1. Digital presence errors: having no website or one that does not work
  2. Local SEO errors: being invisible on Google Maps
  3. Google Business Profile errors: the abandoned listing
  4. Social media and digital communication errors
  5. Strategy errors: spending without measuring or planning
  6. Frequently asked questions

After working with dozens of freelancers across Spain's Comunidad Valenciana -- carpenters in Elda, electricians in Alicante, dentists in Valencia, plumbers in Elche -- we have seen the same digital marketing mistakes over and over again. Mistakes that cost clients, time and money. The good news is they all have solutions, and in many cases the fix is simpler than you might think. This article is a complete tour of the 10 most common errors that hold back freelancers' digital growth.

Digital presence errors: having no website or one that does not work

In 2026, 65% of freelancers in Spain still do not have a website, losing between 5 and 15 calls per month from potential clients. Equally serious is having a website that does not convert: no contact form, no WhatsApp button, not mobile-friendly, or taking more than 3 seconds to load. And many freelancers pay 2,000-5,000 euros for a website they could have for a fraction of the cost.

Local SEO errors: being invisible on Google Maps

Most freelancers do not even know what local SEO is. They have a website that does not mention their city and competes against all of Spain instead of the 5-10 competitors in their area. Furthermore, without local backlinks from other websites pointing to yours, Google cannot know that your business is relevant in your zone.

Google Business Profile errors: the abandoned listing

Many freelancers do not have a Google listing or have not claimed the one Google created automatically. Others claim it, add their name and phone number, and abandon it forever: no photos, no posts, no review responses. 93% of clients read reviews before hiring, and if your competitor has 45 reviews at 4.8 stars while you have 3, guess who they will call.

Social media and digital communication errors

An Instagram profile with the last post from 8 months ago is worse than having no social media at all because it signals abandonment. Choose ONE social network and do it well. Additionally, most freelancers use their personal WhatsApp to serve clients, mixing conversations without auto-replies. WhatsApp Business is free and completely transforms the experience.

Strategy errors: spending without measuring or planning

Some freelancers spend 200-500 euros per month on advertising when their website has no contact form and their Google listing is not optimised. Others measure nothing: without Google Analytics or Search Console, they navigate blindly. And many lack patience: SEO takes 3-6 months to bear fruit. The key is combining short-term actions with long-term investments.

These 10 mistakes are common but they all have solutions. Start with the basics: a professional website, a complete Google listing, WhatsApp Business set up and a local SEO strategy. With these pillars in place, every euro invested in digital marketing will deliver a real return. Digital marketing is a marathon, not a sprint.

Frequently asked questions

What is the most common digital marketing mistake among freelancers?
The most common mistake is having no digital presence at all, or depending entirely on social media without owning a website. 65% of freelancers in Spain have no website, which means they are invisible to the 73% of consumers who search on Google before hiring. The website is the foundation everything else is built on.
How much should a freelancer invest in digital marketing?
The general recommendation is to set aside between 5% and 10% of your revenue for marketing. For a freelancer billing 30,000 euros a year, that would be between 125 and 250 euros a month. That said, you can start with far less: a professional website from 15 euros a month, and optimising your Google listing costs nothing. What matters is starting with the basics and scaling as results come in.
Does social media replace a website?
No. Social media complements a website, it does not replace one. On social media you control neither the algorithm nor the rules. If Instagram or Facebook change their policies, your visibility can collapse overnight. Your website is yours, it appears on Google around the clock, and it is where customers decide to get in touch. Social media attracts; the website converts.
Do I need to be on every social network?
No, quite the opposite. Being on five networks and tending to none is worse than being on one and doing it properly. Choose the network where your customers are: Instagram suits visual businesses (renovations, hair salons), Facebook suits local shops with a community, and LinkedIn suits professional services. One well-run network beats five abandoned ones.
How long does digital marketing take to produce results?
It depends on the channel. Google Ads can generate calls from day one, but it costs money. Local SEO takes between three and six months to show visible results, but those results last and cost nothing. Social media needs consistency over at least three months to build an audience. The key is to combine a short-term strategy (Google Ads or WhatsApp) with a long-term one (SEO, website and Google Business).
