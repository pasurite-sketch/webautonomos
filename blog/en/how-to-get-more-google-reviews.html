How to Get More Google Reviews for Your Business: Complete Guide 2026

You do good work, your customers are satisfied, and yet your Google listing shows three reviews while the competitor next door has fifty. The problem is almost never the quality of the service: it is that nobody asks for the review. In this guide we bring together the methods that genuinely work for gathering more reviews, ethically and without pestering anyone: from the direct link to NFC cards, by way of message templates and handling negative reviews.

Google reviews are the most essential factor for new customers to choose you. A business with 50 reviews at 4.5 stars will always beat the business with 5 reviews, even if they're 5 stars. In this complete guide we've compiled the best tips to get more reviews ethically and effectively to increase your business visibility.

88% of people trust Google reviews as much as personal recommendations. For freelancers and small businesses, getting more Google reviews is the best digital marketing strategy that exists. Each positive review is a potential customer choosing you over the competition, helping to attract more customers to your company.

Why Google reviews are essential for your business

Google uses reviews as a key ranking factor for local SEO in the Local Pack (the 3 results with map). More positive reviews means better results in local search engines. Additionally, a good rating increases CTR on your Google Profile or Google My Business.

The data speaks for itself: 93% of people read reviews before choosing a product or service, 72% don't act until reading positive reviews, and businesses with more than 4 stars receive 12% more clicks. Google reviews are the center of your digital presence.

How Google's review algorithm works

Google values three main aspects: quantity (more is better), quality (high average score), and frequency (recent reviews weigh more). A constant flow of new reviews indicates that your company is active and satisfied customers continue leaving reviews.

The content of reviews also matters. Detailed reviews with relevant keywords (electrician, plumber, fast service...) help Google understand your business and show you in more specific searches, improving your local SEO results.

How to create your direct review link

The first step to get more reviews is to make it easy for your customers. Create a direct review link that takes people directly to your business's rating page. From your Google My Business dashboard, go to Home > Request reviews and copy the short review link.

Set up and verify your Google profile

Before asking for reviews, make sure your Google Profile is complete. Set up and verify your profile with all your company information: hours, address, phone, photos and service description. A complete profile generates more trust and customers who leave reviews will find your business more easily.

You can also create the review link manually: search for your business on Google Maps, click 'Write a review' and copy the URL. Use a shortener like bit.ly to make it easy to share. This link is your most valuable tool for getting more Google reviews.

The perfect time to ask for reviews

Timing is crucial when you're looking to get more reviews. Ask for the review right after completing a successful job, when the customer is most satisfied. The best moments are: immediately after finishing the service, when the customer expresses satisfaction, or after successfully handling an issue.

Don't wait days because enthusiasm cools down. Response rate drops 50% after 24 hours. Incorporate review requests into your regular job closing process. This is one of the methods that has worked best for thousands of businesses.

Strategies to ask for reviews: Templates and effective methods

There are multiple strategies to ask for reviews that have worked for thousands of companies. Below we present the most effective ones with personalized requests.

Via WhatsApp or SMS (the most effective method)

Sending personalized requests via WhatsApp or SMS is the method that has given the best results. Example message: 'Hi [name], I'm glad you were satisfied with the work. If you have a moment, it would really help me if you left your review on Google. Here's the direct review link: [link]. Thanks!'

Always personalize with the customer's name. Response rate via WhatsApp and SMS is much higher than via email.

In person: personalized requests

'I'm glad everything went well. Reviews from satisfied customers like you really help others find me. Would you mind leaving me a Google review? I'll send you the link right now.' Be natural and sincere, not pushy. If the customer says yes, send the review link immediately.

Via email

Subject: 'Your feedback helps us improve the experience'. Content: Brief thanks, direct request, visible direct review link, and professional signature with author or team details. Send it the same day or maximum 24 hours after the service.

NFC cards and stands to get reviews

NFC cards and stands are one of the most innovative tools to get reviews. The customer just needs to bring their phone close to the NFC card and the review page automatically opens. It's the fastest way to make it easy.

Where to place NFC cards: at the counter or customer service center, next to the cash register, on the work table when finishing the service, in the work vehicle. NFC cards and stands cost between €15 and €30 and are an investment with great results.

Using QR codes to get more Google reviews

QR codes are another effective method to get more reviews. Generate a QR with your direct review link using free tools like qr-code-generator.com. The customer just needs to scan with their phone.

Strategic locations for the QR: business cards, invoices and quotes, stickers on the work vehicle, posters in your premises or work center, thank you cards.

Tools to get reviews and management software

The Google My Business dashboard is free and indispensable for any company. It lets you see every Google review, reply to them and get basic statistics. Turn on notifications so you can follow up new opinions quickly.

If you're looking to go further, there are specialized software like Trustpilot, Podium or Birdeye. These AI platforms automate sending personalized requests via email and SMS, track responses, and help you manage all your reviews from a single center.

How to respond to positive reviews

Responding to positive reviews is an essential part of review management. Thank them personally mentioning the customer's name, reference the work done, and maintain a professional but friendly tone. A 2-3 line response is enough.

Example: 'Thank you so much for your review, [name]. It was a pleasure helping you with [type of work]. We'll be here whenever you need us! - The [company name] team'

How to respond to a negative review

A negative review is inevitable, but your response can turn it into an opportunity to improve the experience for future customers. Stay calm and wait a few hours before responding to negative reviews. Never argue publicly or use a defensive tone.

Structure for responding: thank them for the feedback and comment, apologize if appropriate, offer to handle the issue concretely, invite them to continue the conversation privately.

Example: 'We're sorry your experience wasn't what you expected. We'd like to understand what happened and handle the issue. Could you contact us at [phone] to discuss it personally? We want to improve your experience and make sure you're satisfied. - Customer service team'

What to do with fake or spam reviews

If you receive a fake review (from someone who isn't a real customer) or spam, you can report it from your dashboard. Click the three dots on the comment and select 'Report review'. Google will review whether it violates their policies.

Meanwhile, respond professionally indicating you have no record of that customer. This shows other users the review may not be legitimate. Google takes between 3 days and 3 weeks to review reports.

Mistakes you should avoid when asking for reviews

Never buy fake reviews: Google detects them and can penalize or remove your Google Profile. Customers also detect suspicious patterns and you lose credibility. The risk isn't worth it.

Don't offer discounts in exchange for reviews: it goes against Google My Business policies. You can thank after receiving a review, but don't make it conditional. Also don't selectively ask for reviews only from customers you know are satisfied.

Ideal frequency of new reviews to increase visibility

Google values a constant flow more than occasional spikes. The ideal is to get more reviews regularly: between 2 and 5 new ones per month for a small business. This demonstrates continuous activity and satisfied customers.

If you have many satisfied customers but few Google reviews, start by asking the most recent ones. Don't bombard everyone at once, distribute personalized requests to maintain a natural flow and increase your business visibility sustainably.

Getting more Google reviews is constant work, not a one-time action. Review management is one of the most profitable digital marketing strategies for any business. Each positive review is a commercial helping to attract more customers 24 hours a day. With the tips to get more reviews from this guide, you can go from having few reviews to becoming the best-rated business in your area.

Frequently asked questions

How many reviews do I need to increase my visibility on Google?
There's no magic number, but try to surpass your local competition. Generally, 20-30 Google reviews with a rating above 4 stars already position you well in the Local Pack. The essential thing is to maintain a constant flow of new reviews to increase your business visibility.
Can I buy reviews on Google My Business?
No, it's prohibited by Google and they can penalize or remove your Google Profile. Also, people detect fake reviews and you lose credibility. Only real reviews from satisfied customers work long-term.
How do I respond to a negative review without making it worse?
Stay calm, thank them for the comment, apologize if appropriate, offer to handle the issue concretely and propose continuing the conversation privately. Never argue publicly. Your response is read by other potential customers looking for more information.
How do I create the direct review link for my customers?
From your Google My Business dashboard, go to Home > Request reviews and copy the short review link. You can also search for your business on Google Maps, click 'Write review' and copy the URL. Use a shortener like bit.ly to make it easy to share.
What's the best time to ask for a review?
Right after completing a successful job, when the customer expresses satisfaction. Response rate drops 50% after 24 hours. Ask for the review in person, via WhatsApp, SMS or email the same day of service.
Can I offer discounts in exchange for Google reviews?
No, it goes against Google My Business policies. You can thank a customer after they leave a review, but you can't make discounts or gifts conditional. Google can penalize your Google Profile if they detect this practice.
How do I remove a fake or spam review?
Report the comment from your dashboard: click the three dots on the review and select 'Report review'. Google will review if it violates their policies in 3 days to 3 weeks. Meanwhile, respond professionally indicating you have no record of that customer.
Are old reviews still useful for SEO?
Yes, but Google gives more weight to recent reviews. A constant flow of new reviews (2-5 per month) indicates your business is active. Reviews from more than 1 year ago have less impact on local SEO results.
Should I respond to all reviews or just negative ones?
Respond to all. Responses to positive reviews show you value customer service and encourage other customers to leave reviews. Responses to negative ones demonstrate professionalism. Google also values active interaction for SEO.
How do I use NFC cards and stands to get reviews?
NFC cards and stands are very effective tools to get reviews. Place them at your customer service center or counter. The customer just brings their phone close and the review page automatically opens. It's the fastest way to make it easy.
What do I do if a satisfied customer doesn't want to leave a review?
Respect their decision, don't insist. Some people value their privacy. Making it easy with direct review link, NFC cards or QR helps, but don't pressure. Focus on customers who are willing to help you.
Do Google reviews affect my Maps positioning?
Yes, Google reviews are one of the most essential factors for SEO on Google Maps and the Local Pack. Quantity, quality and frequency of new reviews directly influence your local business visibility.
Can I ask for reviews via WhatsApp, SMS or email?
Yes, sending personalized requests via WhatsApp, SMS or email is very effective. Include the customer's name, thank them for the work done and include the direct review link. WhatsApp and SMS have a higher open rate than email.
How long do reviews take to appear published?
Reviews usually appear immediately or within a few hours. In some cases, Google may hold them for review up to 24-48 hours. If a review doesn't appear after 3 days, it may have been filtered by Google's systems.
What review management software do you recommend?
For most small businesses, the free Google My Business dashboard is enough. If you are after automation, AI-driven platforms such as Trustpilot, Podium or Birdeye help you manage personalised requests, follow up and reply to reviews from a single hub.
