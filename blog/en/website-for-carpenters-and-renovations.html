If you are a carpenter or run a renovation company in Spain's Comunidad Valenciana, you know your best business card is the quality of your work. But if that work is not visible online, you are losing clients every day. A well-designed website for carpenters and renovation companies turns your completed projects into a constant flow of quote requests. In this guide we explain exactly what your website needs to showcase your work, build trust and win new clients in Valencia, Alicante, Elda, Elche and across the region.

Why a website for carpenters and renovation companies is no longer optional

The carpentry and renovation sector in Spain billed over 42 billion euros in 2025, and the trend keeps rising. But 73% of clients looking for a renovation company start with Google. If you do not have a website, you simply do not exist for those clients. The difference between a carpenter with a website and one without is straightforward: one receives calls from new clients every week, while the other depends entirely on word of mouth.

The visual portfolio: the section that generates the most clients

In a carpentry or renovation business, a picture is worth a thousand words and more than a thousand euros in advertising. Your online portfolio is the most powerful sales tool you can have. 87% of potential renovation clients say photos of previous projects are the decisive factor when requesting a quote. Nothing sells better than a good before-and-after comparison.

What sections should your carpentry or renovation website have

An effective renovation company website does not need twenty pages. It needs the right pages, well-structured and with the information clients are looking for: a homepage that hooks visitors within 3 seconds, a detailed services page, client testimonials and reviews, and a quote request form with the ability to attach photos.

How to optimise your renovation website for local Google search

For clients to find you when they search for carpenter in Valencia or renovation company Elche, you need web design geared towards local SEO. Every page of your website should mention your location, include your city in the title, in the H1 heading and in at least two H2 subheadings. If you work in several cities, create a dedicated page for each one.

Real cases: carpenters and renovators thriving with their website

Antonio, a carpenter in Elda, spent 15 years working solely through referrals. When he created his website with a gallery of custom furniture, he started receiving enquiries from clients in Petrer, Novelda and Monovar. Within six months his turnover grew by 35%. Reformas Martinez in Alicante went from 2-3 quotes per month to 12-15 thanks to their before-and-after portfolio.

You do not need a huge website -- you need a well-thought-out one. A powerful visual portfolio, clear sections, a contact form with WhatsApp integration, and local Google optimisation. With these elements, your carpentry or renovation website becomes your best salesperson, working 24 hours a day.

Frequently asked questions

Which photos should I put on my renovation website?
Before-and-after photos of each project are the most effective. Include at least three to five projects with good-quality images — decent lighting, no clutter. Renovation customers need to see real results before requesting a quote. Photos of your workshop or your team also build trust.
Does my renovation firm need an online portfolio?
Yes. A portfolio is the single most important element of a carpentry or renovation website. 87% of customers look at previous work before requesting a quote. Your portfolio replaces the paper catalogue: it shows what you can do better than any text. Organise it by project type to make browsing easier.
Can a website win me customers if I already use social media?
Social media and a website complement each other rather than replacing one another. On social media you depend on the algorithm, and your posts vanish within 24 to 48 hours. Your website is always available, it appears on Google when someone searches for your service, and you control the content. The ideal is to use social media to attract and the website to turn visits into quote requests.
How can my website generate automatic quote requests?
Include a simple contact form asking for name, phone, project type and a brief description. Add a WhatsApp button visible on every page as well. Renovation customers prefer quick contact. Make sure the form works properly on mobile, because 70% of local searches are made on a phone.
