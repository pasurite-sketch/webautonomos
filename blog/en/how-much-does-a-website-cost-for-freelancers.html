One of the most frequent questions we receive is: how much does it cost to create a website? The answer can vary considerably depending on many factors. In this complete guide we analyze all the options in the Spanish market so that freelancers and small businesses can make the best decision.

In 2026, having a digital presence is no longer optional for any freelancer or SME that wants to grow. But web design prices can vary enormously: from €10 per month to more than €6,000. Depending on various factors such as the type of website, features, and who develops it, the total cost can multiply.

Factors that influence website price

Before analyzing the different options, it's important to understand what factors cause web design and development prices to vary so much. Knowing these aspects will help you define what you really need and avoid overpaying.

The type of website is fundamental: a basic informational website is not the same as a complete online store. A personal website or portfolio is the simplest and most economical, while an online store with product catalog and payment gateway is more complex and costly.

The level of customization also affects: a standard template from €200 is economical and fast, a customized template according to your specific needs costs between €500 and €1,200, and a complete custom design with unique branding goes from €1,000 to €6,000 or more.

Each additional feature increases the price: contact form (basic included), booking system (€100-500 extra), online store (€300-2,000 extra), social media integrations, professional email, premium security and SEO plugins, and multilingual (€200-800 extra).

Design agencies: €1,000 - €6,000

Traditional web design agencies are the most expensive option on the market. A design agency in Spain charges between €1,000 and €6,000 for a corporate website, and the price can exceed €10,000 if you need an online store or advanced digital marketing features.

Advantages include fully customized and unique design, expert team in design, development and marketing, custom features according to your specific needs, long-term professional guarantee and support, and great capacity for complex projects.

Disadvantages are very high prices for freelancers and small businesses, long delivery times (weeks or months of development), maintenance and hosting separate (€50-200/month additional), total dependence on the agency for any changes, and budgets that can grow during the project.

Freelance designers: €500 - €1,200

Hiring a freelance web designer can be more economical than an agency, with prices between €500 and €1,200 for a corporate website. Some less experienced professionals offer websites from €800, while the most experienced can charge up to €3,000.

Advantages are that it's more economical than a design agency, direct and personal contact with the professional, greater flexibility in page design, shorter delivery times, and easy communication and project tracking.

Disadvantages include highly variable quality depending on the designer, hosting and web domain separate (€50-100/year), risk that the freelancer disappears or isn't available, complicated long-term maintenance, and they don't always master SEO or digital marketing.

DIY platforms: €10 - €30/month

Wix, Squarespace or WordPress.com are tools that allow you to create a website yourself with visual editors. Plans range from €10 to €30 per month. The idea is that you can design and develop a website without technical knowledge.

Advantages are affordable monthly price if you have time, total control over your website, you can make changes whenever you want, you don't depend on anyone for simple updates, and many free templates available.

Disadvantages are that it requires time and learning curve, unprofessional result without design experience, limited SEO on basic plans, frustration if you don't master the tools, and total cost increases with premium plugins.

Hidden costs you should know

Many web design quotes don't include all the expenses necessary to keep your website running. A .es web domain costs between €8 and €20/year, .com between €10 and €15 per year. Hosting service varies: basic shared €60/year, medium quality €100-150/year, premium €200-500/year.

Maintenance includes security and plugin updates, periodic data backups, SSL certificate (free or up to €100/year), and variable technical support. A website with €800 design can end up costing more than €1,000 the first year if you add everything up.

Which option to choose for your case?

Choose a design agency if your company has a high budget for digital marketing, you need very specific features or complex integrations, you want completely unique branding and design, or you're going to launch an online store with thousands of products.

Choose a freelance designer if you have a medium budget (€500-1,200), time to search for and manage the designer, you don't mind managing hosting and domain separately, or you know a trusted professional.

Choose DIY if you have free time and like learning new tools, you don't get frustrated easily with technology, your goal is a personal website or simple blog, or you enjoy the process of designing yourself.
