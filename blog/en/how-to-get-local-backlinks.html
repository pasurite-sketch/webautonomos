Local backlinks: how to get links that boost your business

In the world of local SEO, local backlinks are like word-of-mouth recommendations in digital form. When the website of Elche City Council, the Alicante Chamber of Commerce or the Informacion newspaper links to your website, Google reads it as a trust signal: this business is legitimate and relevant in its area. In this guide we show you concrete strategies to get quality local backlinks without spending a single euro on purchased links.

What are local backlinks and why does Google value them so highly

A backlink is a link from another website to yours, but not all backlinks are equal. For Google, a link from Valencia City Council is worth infinitely more than one from an anonymous blog. Backlinks account for 28% of the weight in Google's Map Pack ranking. In other words, nearly a third of your visibility on Google Maps depends on the links pointing to your website.

Public institutions and business associations

Institutional websites are the most valuable source of local backlinks. City councils in Valencia, Alicante, Elche and Elda maintain directories of local businesses. Chambers of Commerce have directories of associated businesses linking to their websites. Professional and trade associations also offer member directories that strengthen your E-E-A-T signals for Google.

Local press, blogs and community events

Digital newspapers such as Levante-EMV, Informacion and Las Provincias have extremely high domain authority. You can earn links through business openings, special projects, expert opinions or local event sponsorship. Local blogs and city guides are also valuable sources of backlinks.

Suppliers, clients and local collaborations

Your professional contact network is another source of backlinks you are probably not using. Write testimonials for your supplier's website, create cross-referral networks with other professionals and ask satisfied clients to mention you on their websites. Supplement these with quality local directories such as Paginas Amarillas, QDQ and Habitissimo.

Mistakes that destroy your local link building strategy

Avoid buying links, mass link exchanges, spam directories, repetitive anchor texts and acquiring many links at once. Laura, a physiotherapist in Valencia, obtained only 8 quality local backlinks over 6 months and moved from page three to page one on Google for physiotherapist Valencia. Quality always beats quantity.

Local backlinks are one of the most powerful tools in local SEO. You do not need hundreds of links: with 10-20 quality backlinks from institutions, local press, suppliers and associations you can compete for the top positions on Google in your area. The key is patience and legitimacy.

Frequently asked questions

What is a local backlink, and why does it matter for SEO?
A local backlink is a link from a website in your geographic area to your page. It might come from a town council, a chamber of commerce, a local newspaper or a nearby supplier. Google reads these links as votes of confidence confirming that your business is relevant in that area. A single backlink from Valencia city council can carry more weight than dozens of links from generic directories.
How many local backlinks do I need to rank my website?
There is no magic number. For a freelancer working in a mid-sized town such as Elda or Elche, between 10 and 20 quality local backlinks can be enough to compete for the top Google positions. What counts is not the quantity but the quality and relevance of each link. One link from the Alicante Chamber of Commerce is worth more than 50 from unknown blogs.
Is buying backlinks risky for my business?
Yes. Buying links is a practice Google actively penalises. If it detects a pattern of bought links — private blog networks, link farms or mass swapping — it can penalise your site and remove it from the results. Recovering from a penalty can take months. It is far better to spend that time earning five good local links legitimately.
How long does a backlink take to take effect?
It depends on the authority of the site linking to you and how often Google crawls it. A link from a local newspaper can take effect within one or two weeks. A link from a business directory can take one to three months to be found and valued. The point is that link building is a medium to long-term strategy: the results accumulate over time.
Can I earn local backlinks without having a website?
Backlinks are links pointing at your website, so you need a site to receive them. That said, local citations — mentions of your business name, address and phone number in directories — work in a similar way even without a direct link. If you do not have a website yet, start by building one and then work on backlinks. The website is the destination; the backlinks are the roads.
