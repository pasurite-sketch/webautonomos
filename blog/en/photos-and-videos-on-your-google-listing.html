Photos and videos on your Google listing: why they double your clicks

  4. Technical requirements and best practices for your images

  6. Frequently asked questions

Your Google Business Profile is your digital shopfront. And as with any shopfront, what people see is what sells. Photos and videos on your Google listing are not a decorative add-on -- they are the difference between a client calling you or your competitor. According to Google data, businesses with over 100 photos receive 520% more calls than those with none. If you are a freelancer in Spain's Comunidad Valenciana, this guide teaches you exactly which images to upload, in what format and how often to multiply your clicks.

Why photos on your Google listing are so important

When someone searches for electrician in Valencia or hairdresser Alicante on Google, the first thing they see is the Map Pack featuring the main photo. Listings with images on Google Business receive 42% more direction requests and 35% more clicks to the website. 60% of consumers prefer businesses with photos on Google Maps over those without.

The 7 types of photos your Google listing needs

Google categorises business profile photos into types: cover photo, business logo, exterior shots, interior shots, team photos, products or services in action, and atmosphere and details. Covering all of them sends positive signals to the algorithm and helps clients get to know your business before visiting.

Videos on Google Business Profile: the format that makes the difference

If photos double your clicks, videos on Google Maps triple them. Very few local businesses use videos on their listing yet, which means you have a huge opportunity to stand out. Google allows videos of up to 30 seconds, 75MB and a minimum resolution of 720p. With your phone and good lighting you can record videos that make a real difference.

Technical requirements and best practices for your images

Google accepts JPG and PNG formats with a minimum resolution of 720x720 pixels, though 1200x900 is ideal. Maximum file size is 5MB per image. Avoid blurry photos, excessive filters, stock photos and text overlays. Before uploading, rename files with descriptive keywords such as carpentry-custom-furniture-valencia.jpg.

Publishing strategy: when and how many photos to upload

Google rewards consistent activity. Follow a weekly plan: upload a team photo on Monday, a completed project on Wednesday and an ambience shot on Friday. With 3 photos per week, within three months your listing will have more visual content than 90% of your competitors. Enable your phone's geolocation to send local SEO signals with every photo.

Photos and videos on your Google listing are the most cost-effective investment you can make in local marketing. They are free, easy to create with your phone and they multiply your clicks and calls. Start today with 15-20 quality photos and maintain a rhythm of 3 weekly uploads to dominate the Map Pack in your area.

Frequently asked questions

How many photos should I upload to my Google listing?
Google recommends a minimum of 10 photos for a listing to count as complete. That said, businesses with more than 25 photos receive 35% more clicks than those with fewer than 10. The ideal is to start with 15 to 20 good photos and then add two or three new ones every week to keep your profile active.
What format and resolution should the photos be?
Google accepts JPG and PNG. The recommended resolution is at least 720x720 pixels, though 1200x900 is ideal. The maximum size per photo is 5MB. Avoid blurred or very dark photos, and heavy filters. The photos should represent your business faithfully.
Can I upload videos to Google Business Profile?
Yes. Google Business Profile accepts videos of up to 30 seconds and 75MB. Videos need a minimum resolution of 720p. They can show your premises, your team at work, or a service in action. Businesses with videos receive 20% more interactions than those with photos alone.
How often should I update the photos on my listing?
Ideally you should add new photos at least once a week. Google rewards active profiles with better rankings. If your business is seasonal, refresh the photos with each season. And if you have refitted or changed your premises, upload the photos immediately so customers see the most current version.
Can customers upload photos to my Google listing?
Yes, any Google user can upload photos to your listing. That can be positive when customers share good experiences, but it can also be a problem if they upload low-quality or irrelevant images. You cannot delete user photos directly, but you can report them to Google if they breach the policies. The best strategy is to upload so many good photos of your own that the user ones fade into the background.
