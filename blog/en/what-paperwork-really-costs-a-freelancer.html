Time spent on admin: what it costs a small business in Spain

Admin time is easy to underestimate: nobody invoices you for it, and it never appears on an expense line. Yet in Spain, official paperwork alone takes a self-employed worker more than five working weeks a year: 226 hours on average, according to ATA. Here is what that figure leaves out, what it really costs your business and how to win some of it back.

How much time does a small business spend on admin?

For a self-employed worker in Spain, official paperwork alone takes 226 hours a year, according to ATA. Quotes, invoices, emails and chasing late payers come on top, so the true total is higher.

Where the 226-hour figure comes from

ATA, the Federación Nacional de Asociaciones de Trabajadores Autónomos (a national federation of self-employed workers' associations), published the figure in August 2026.

It counts only dealings with public administrations: quarterly tax returns, registrations, deregistrations and formal requests.

What it costs: €3,390 a year

ATA puts the cost at €3,390 per person, valuing each hour at €15. If you bill €35 or €40 an hour, your actual cost is two to three times higher.

Across some 3.4 million autónomos, the collective bill comes to around €11,770 million annually.

What the ATA figure leaves out

Running a business also brings admin tasks that never reach a timesheet:

  • Quotes and invoices: the quote you redo for the third time, the invoices you retype from delivery notes.
  • Chasing payments: unpaid invoices you follow up whenever a spare moment turns up, while your cash flow waits.
  • Emails and messages: confirming visits, answering customers, hunting for the agreed price.
  • Bookkeeping and banking: receipts, bank statements and online banking to see who has paid.

Each one looks minor. Together, they decide how much is left for paid work.

Why going digital has not cut the paperwork

According to research published in 2026 by Qonto and IO Investigación, 23 % of Spanish SMEs with a high level of digitalisation spend more than ten days a month on administrative tasks, against 9 % of the least digitalised.

The same research points to the cause: 75 % run more than one platform to manage their finances, and 29 % run four or more. Effort is lost between systems, typing the same data into several places.

More technology does not necessarily mean less admin.

The official paperwork of an autónomo in Spain

For business owners in Spain, official paperwork revolves around the Tax Agency (Agencia Tributaria, often called Hacienda) and Social Security:

TaskBodyWhen
RegistrationAgencia Tributaria and Social SecurityBefore you start trading
VAT (IVA) and income tax (IRPF) returnsAgencia TributariaEvery quarter, plus annual returns
Cuota de autónomos, based on real incomeSocial SecurityEvery month
NotificationsTax Agency and Social SecurityWhenever one is sent
DeregistrationTax Agency and Social SecurityWhen you stop trading

Social Security notifies you electronically, and the Tax Agency can too. A notification you don't open before the deadline is treated as delivered, so switch on the email alerts.

What admin really costs your business

Admin costs a business owner twice: the time it takes, valued at what they could bill, and the paid jobs it pushes out.

How to work out your own figure

Multiply how many times a day you do a task by how long it takes and by what your time is worth, then by 240 working days. That annual cost shows you what to change first.

Signs that admin is taking over

  • You send invoices long after finishing the job.
  • Half of your Sunday goes on catching up with emails.
  • You struggle to find time for quotes and prospects.
  • Paperwork spills past the working day and into your work-life balance.

How to reduce the paperwork

Organise first, then delegate, and only then hand repetitive tasks to software. The first steps cost little and suit sole traders and teams alike.

Organise your week

  • Note the tax calendar in January, with reminders before each deadline.
  • Book a weekly slot to issue invoices, file receipts and check notifications.
  • Use a bank account just for the business and photograph each receipt straight away.
  • Save templates for quotes and repeated emails; WhatsApp Business also has quick replies and away messages.

Good time management is routine, not willpower: grouping similar tasks leaves more time to focus on customers.

Use one tool instead of several

One invoicing and management program for quotes, invoices and expenses, a folder you share with your gestoría and a backup cover the basics. The right program is the one you will actually use: if it offers a free trial, test it on a real week of invoices.

A digital certificate or Cl@ve lets you complete official procedures online instead of at a local office.

Delegate to a gestoría

A gestoría (an adviser who handles tax and Social Security paperwork) can process your registration and file your taxes. Issuing invoices, keeping receipts and sending the information on schedule remain your job, and so does the responsibility.

Before you hire one, ask the right questions:

  • which services are included, from registration to quarterly taxes and, if you have staff, payroll;
  • who files each tax and how you receive proof of filing;
  • what each service costs.

When is automation worth it?

When a task meets three conditions:

  • it comes up often;
  • each repetition looks alike;
  • the information is already stored electronically.

A task that takes ten minutes daily does not justify a custom build: the maintenance eats the saving.

Frequently asked questions

Where does the 226-hour figure come from?
From the Federación Nacional de Asociaciones de Trabajadores Autónomos (ATA), published in August 2026. It covers dealings with public administrations only, not the rest of the administrative tasks in a business.
What taxes does an autónomo file every quarter?
In general, a VAT (IVA) return and an income tax (IRPF) instalment, plus withholding returns if they pay rent or professionals' invoices. Annual summaries and the income tax return complete the calendar. The Agencia Tributaria publishes the current forms and dates.
