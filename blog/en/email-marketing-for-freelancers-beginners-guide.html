Email marketing for freelancers: start without spending a euro

📑 Article contents

Email marketing for freelancers is one of the most profitable and least utilized tools in digital marketing. While everyone talks about social media and TikTok, email remains the channel with the highest return on investment: for every euro invested, it generates an average of 36 euros. And the best part is you can start completely for free. Whether you are a plumber in Valencia, an electrician in Alicante, a hairdresser in Elda, or a physiotherapist in Elche, this guide teaches you how to create your first newsletter, retain your existing customers, and get referrals that turn into new jobs.

Why email marketing is perfect for freelancers

Email marketing generates an average return of 36 euros for every euro invested, according to a Litmus study. It works even better for small businesses because you have a personal relationship with your customers. Unlike social media, your email list is yours: it does not depend on algorithms or third-party platforms that can change the rules at any moment.

Free email marketing tools: which one to choose

You do not need to spend a single euro to get started. Brevo (formerly Sendinblue) offers unlimited contacts and up to 300 emails per day for free. Mailchimp offers up to 500 contacts and 1,000 sends per month. MailerLite allows up to 1,000 subscribers and 12,000 emails per month. If you plan to grow, Brevo is the best option thanks to its unlimited contacts.

How to build your contact list from scratch

As a freelancer, you already have more contacts than you think. Gather your existing customers from invoices and WhatsApp, get GDPR consent, add a subscription form on your website offering something in return, and capture emails at every interaction (quotes, invoices, conversations). Your list will grow naturally over time.

What to send: 6 types of emails that work for freelancers

The 6 most effective email types are: an automatic welcome email (up to 80% open rate), a monthly newsletter with tips and promotions, a seasonal reminder email, a post-job email requesting a Google review, a special offer email with a deadline, and a referral email asking customers to recommend you in exchange for a discount.

How to write emails that get opened and drive action

The subject line is everything: 47% of people decide whether to open an email based solely on the subject. Keep it short and catchy. Personalize whenever possible, be brief (150-300 words), include a clear call to action, and send on Tuesdays or Thursdays between 10 and 11 AM. Carmen, a hairdresser in Elda, achieved a 42% open rate and generated 15 direct appointments in 6 months.

Email marketing is the perfect tool for freelancers to retain customers and generate referrals. You do not need thousands of subscribers: a list of 100 customers who open your emails has more power than 5,000 Instagram followers. Start today with a free tool, send one email per month, and watch how your customers remember you and recommend you to others.

Frequently asked questions

Does email marketing work for freelancers, or is it only for big companies?
Email marketing works particularly well for freelancers. Unlike a large company, a freelancer has a close relationship with their customers, which means the emails get read more attentively. A plumber sending a reminder about the annual boiler service, or a hairdresser announcing a promotion, reach open rates of up to 40% — far above those of big brands.
Which free email marketing tool is best to start with?
For a freelancer starting out, Brevo (formerly Sendinblue) is the best free option. Its free plan allows 300 emails a day with unlimited contacts. Mailchimp also has a free plan for up to 500 contacts and 1,000 sends a month. If you only need to send the occasional email rather than regular newsletters, Mailchimp is enough. If you expect to grow, Brevo gives you more headroom without paying.
Do I need permission to email my customers?
Yes, it is required by GDPR and, in Spain, by the LSSI. You need the recipients explicit consent before sending commercial email. The simplest way is to add a tick box to your contact or quote form saying something like: I agree to receive commercial communications from [your business]. You must also include an unsubscribe link in every email.
How often should I email my customers?
For a freelancer, once a month is a good rhythm to begin with. It is enough that they do not forget you, and not so much that they tire of you. If you have news or a seasonal promotion, you can send an extra one. Regularity is what matters: one email a month for a year beats four emails in January and then silence until December.
What open rate is normal in email marketing?
The average email open rate in Spain sits between 20% and 25%. Freelancers with small lists and a close relationship with their customers usually see higher figures, from 30% to 45%. If your open rate drops below 15%, your subject lines are probably not compelling, or you are emailing too often. A good subject line can be the difference between 15% and 40%.
