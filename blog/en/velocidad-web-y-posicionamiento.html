Why your website's speed affects your Google ranking

You have a beautiful website, but it takes forever to load. The problem is that, while the visitor waits, they've already left, and Google knows it. Your website's speed isn't a technical detail: it directly affects your ranking on Google and how many clients stay. In this guide we explain, without the jargon, why speed matters so much and what you can do about it.

Why Google cares so much about speed

Google wants to give its users the best possible experience, and a slow website is a bad experience. That's why, for years, loading speed has been an official ranking factor: between two similar websites, the faster one tends to appear higher. Google even measures specific speed and stability signals (the so-called Core Web Vitals) to decide which websites deserve the top spots.

Every second counts: speed and your clients

The studies are clear: most visitors abandon a website that takes more than 3 seconds to load. If your website is slow, you don't just lose rankings on Google, you directly lose clients who never even get to see what you offer. In a local business, every visit counts: a fast website converts more calls, more bookings and more enquiries. Speed is, literally, money.

What makes a website slow (and how you notice)

The most common causes of a slow website are: heavy, unoptimised images, templates overloaded with features you don't use, too many plugins or scripts, and cheap, overcrowded hosting. It shows most on mobile, where most of your clients visit you: if your website stutters on a phone, you have a problem. The good news is that almost everything can be fixed.

Speed on mobile: where the game is won

More than half of local searches are made on mobile, often on data and without wifi. Google indexes your website prioritising the mobile version, so if your website doesn't load quickly on a phone, your ranking suffers everywhere. A modern website should be built mobile-first: light, fast and easy to use with your thumb. It isn't optional, it's the standard.

How to get a fast website without the hassle

You don't need to be technical to have a fast website: you need it to be well built from the start. That means optimised images, clean code without useless features, and hosting that responds. At WebAutonomos we build light, fast websites by default, hosted on high-performance infrastructure, so speed works in your favour on Google instead of against you.

How to know if your website is fast enough

You can check your website's speed for free with tools like Google PageSpeed Insights: it gives you a score and tells you what's slowing your website down. Pay particular attention to the mobile score. If it's red or orange, you're losing rankings and clients without knowing it. It's the first check you should do if you suspect your website is slow.
