Mandatory electronic invoicing for freelancers in Spain: what you need to know

Electronic invoicing is no longer optional. The Crea y Crece Law will gradually require freelancers and companies to issue in electronic format the invoices they send to other businesses and professionals; invoices to private customers are not covered by this obligation. If you are a freelancer, this guide explains what it is, when it will apply to you, which free tools you can use and how to avoid mistakes. No jargon, no alarmism.

What electronic invoicing is and why it will be mandatory

An electronic invoice is an invoice in a structured format that software can read without human intervention, such as Facturae or UBL, sent through a digital channel. The Crea y Crece Law, approved in 2022, will make it mandatory for transactions between businesses and professionals, to improve transparency, reduce fraud and fight late payment: as well as issuing it, you will have to report the status of each invoice, for example whether it has been accepted or paid. A PDF invoice sent by email is not an electronic invoice under this law. And do not confuse it with Verifactu, which sets the requirements for the software you invoice with: they are two different rules.

Obligation calendar: when it is your turn

The obligation will arrive in stages, according to annual turnover: first, companies with a turnover above 8 million euros a year and, one year later, all other companies and freelancers. The deadlines run from the entry into force of the implementing rules, which have been postponed more than once, so check the current dates on the Spanish Tax Agency (AEAT) website or with your gestor before planning. And even if it is not your turn yet, some business clients may ask you for electronic invoices earlier, so it is worth making sure your software can already issue them.

What format a valid electronic invoice must have

To comply with the Crea y Crece Law, an electronic invoice must be in an accepted structured format, such as Facturae or UBL; include the mandatory details of any invoice (number, date, tax ID and address of issuer and client, description of the service, taxable base, VAT applied and amount); guarantee its authenticity and integrity; and be kept for the legal period (for tax purposes, at least four years; ask your gestor whether you need to keep it longer). If you invoice a public administration through FACe, the Facturae format with an electronic signature is also required. Once the obligation reaches you, an ordinary PDF will not do for invoices between businesses, even if you send it by signed email.

Free tools you can use

FACe is the official platform for invoicing public administrations: free and secure, but limited to the public sector. The Spanish Tax Agency has announced a free invoicing application for freelancers and small businesses with few invoices: check on its website whether it is already available and what it includes. There is also invoicing software with free plans, limited in invoices, users or features, which may be enough for a freelancer with a low volume. Before choosing, check that the software issues invoices in a structured format, that its manufacturer has issued the declaración responsable required by Verifactu and that you can export your data.

How to issue your first electronic invoice step by step

First, get your digital certificate (for individuals, the FNMT issues it free of charge). Second, choose an invoicing tool that issues invoices in a structured format. Third, set up your tax details and those of your clients. Fourth, create the invoice with the description, amount and VAT. Fifth, if you invoice a public administration, sign it electronically from the tool itself. Sixth, send it to the client: if public, through FACe; if private, through the digital channel you agree on. Finally, keep it for the legal period.

Common mistakes that can invalidate your invoice

Confusing a signed PDF invoice with an electronic invoice is a frequent mistake: a structured format is required. Forgetting the electronic signature when you invoice the administration through FACe is another common slip. Using incomplete or incorrect tax details (tax ID, address) means you have to correct the invoice. Not keeping your invoices for the legal period can lead to penalties in an inspection. And if your client is a public administration, send the invoice through FACe, not by email.

What happens if you do not comply: penalties and consequences

The rules provide for penalties for failing to meet the electronic invoicing obligation: check the current amounts with the Spanish Tax Agency or your adviser. In addition, if a public administration requires an electronic invoice from you and you do not issue it, payment may be delayed until you submit it properly, and your business clients may ask you to correct it. The most practical approach is to adapt calmly, before your deadline arrives.

Electronic invoicing will reach all invoices between businesses, but adapting is not complicated: with a digital certificate and suitable invoicing software you can start preparing today. Do not wait for the deadline: adapt now, while there is no pressure, so that you are ready when the obligation arrives.

Frequently asked questions

Is a digitally signed PDF invoice valid?
Not for invoices between businesses once the obligation reaches you: you need a structured format, such as Facturae or UBL, that software can read. Verifactu is a different rule: it sets the requirements for invoicing software, not the format of the invoice.
When does it oblige me as a freelancer?
The obligation arrives in stages: first companies with a turnover above 8 million euros and, one year later, everyone else, freelancers included. Check the current dates with the Spanish Tax Agency or your gestor, and bear in mind that your business clients may ask you for it earlier.
Which free tool do you recommend?
With a low volume, invoicing software with a free plan may be enough, or the free application announced by the Spanish Tax Agency, if it is already available. If you only invoice the public administration, FACe is free. Always check that the tool issues invoices in a structured format.
Do I need an accountant or advisor to issue it?
It is not mandatory, but it helps at first. Once the tool is set up, issuing it is as fast as a traditional invoice.
What about the invoices I already issued in PDF?
They remain valid under the previous regime. The obligation applies to new invoices from the application date that corresponds to you.
