Google Posts: how to publish on your Google listing to attract more customers

Did you know you can publish content directly on your Google listing that appears when someone searches for your business? Google Posts are one of the most powerful and least used features of Google Business Profile. If you are a freelancer in Spain's Valencia, Alicante, Elda, or Elche, posting on your Google listing at least once a week can multiply interactions with your profile and attract more customers. In this guide we show you step by step how to do it, with real examples and the mistakes you must avoid.

What are Google Posts and why you should use them

Google Posts are short publications you can create from your Google Business Profile. They appear directly in Google search results and on Google Maps. According to a BrightLocal study, listings that publish posts regularly receive 35% more clicks on the call button and 25% more route requests on Google Maps. An active listing conveys trust.

The 4 types of Google Posts you can publish

Google Business Profile lets you create four types of posts: Update posts to share news and recent work, Offer posts with an expiration date that create urgency, Event posts with a title and date for open days or talks, and Product posts to highlight specific services with pricing and a description.

How to create a Google Post step by step

Creating a post on your Google listing is easy: from your computer or the mobile app, access your listing, select "Add update", choose the post type, upload a photo (minimum 400x300 pixels), write the text (ideally 150-300 characters), and add an action button like "Call" or "Learn more". The mobile app is the fastest way: you can publish in under 2 minutes.

Content strategy: what to publish each week

Follow a 4-week cycle: Week 1 a recent job with a photo, Week 2 a useful tip related to your profession, Week 3 an offer or promotion with a deadline, and Week 4 a highlighted customer testimonial or review. Repeat this cycle throughout the year, adapting the content to the season.

Mistakes that ruin your Google Posts (and how to avoid them)

The most common mistakes are: posting without an image (70% fewer interactions), texts that are too long, forgetting the action button, posting only when you remember instead of weekly, and generic content without local references. Jaime, a locksmith in Alicante, started posting every Monday and within two months his calls from Google Maps grew from 8 to 14 per month.

Google Posts are a free and powerful tool to attract more customers to your business. Publishing one weekly post with a photo of your work, a brief text, and an action button is enough to keep your listing active and improve your visibility on Google. It takes just 5 minutes a week and can generate work for the entire month.

Frequently asked questions

What are Google Posts?
Google Posts are publications you can create directly from your Google Business Profile listing. They appear when someone searches for your business on Google or in Google Maps. They can carry text, images, action buttons and links. They are a free way of communicating news, offers and services directly in the search results.
How often should I publish Google Posts?
Ideally at least one Google Post a week. Posts stay prominent for seven days before fading into the background, so a weekly publication ensures there is always fresh content visible on your listing. Two or three times a week is better still. Consistency matters more than volume.
Do Google Posts help local SEO?
Yes, indirectly. Google values active, up-to-date listings. Publishing posts regularly tells Google your business is active, which can improve your position in the Map Pack and in local results. Posts also generate more interaction with your listing — clicks, calls, website visits — which is another positive local SEO signal.
Can I schedule Google Posts in advance?
Google Business Profile does not allow native scheduling. There are third-party tools, though, such as Publer, SocialBee and Metricool, that let you schedule publications on your Google listing. Another option is to spend 15 minutes every Monday creating and publishing the weeks post straight from the Google Business Profile app on your phone.
What kind of content works best in Google Posts?
The posts that work best combine an attractive image with short text and a clear action button. Offers with a deadline create urgency, before-and-after posts of completed work build trust, and informative posts with useful advice demonstrate expertise. Avoid generic text: the more specific and local your content, the better it performs.
