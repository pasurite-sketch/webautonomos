Schema LocalBusiness: what it is and why your website needs it

If you have a local business in Spain's Comunidad Valenciana and want Google to understand perfectly who you are, where you are and what you offer, you need to know about Schema LocalBusiness. It is invisible structured data that tells Google in its own language everything about your business. Your customers never see it, but it makes the difference between appearing as a plain result on Google or standing out with opening hours, location, ratings and more.

What is Schema LocalBusiness and how does it work

Schema LocalBusiness is a standardised vocabulary created by Schema.org, a joint project of Google, Bing, Yahoo and Yandex, that allows search engines to understand your website as if it were a database. The recommended way to implement it is through JSON-LD (JavaScript Object Notation for Linked Data), a format Google prefers because it is inserted as an independent block within the HTML, separate from visible content.

Benefits of structured data for your local business

When Google detects valid structured data on your website, it can display rich snippets with star ratings, opening hours, address and price range. These results receive between 20% and 30% more clicks. Schema also improves Google's understanding of your business and strengthens the consistency of your NAP data (Name, Address, Phone) across your entire online presence.

How to create your JSON-LD code step by step

You do not need to be a programmer. A basic Schema LocalBusiness includes: specific business type (Plumber, Electrician, Dentist), exact name, description including your city, full address, GPS coordinates, opening hours, service area and price range. You can use free generators like Merkle's tool to create the code without errors.

How to implement Schema on your website without being a programmer

If your website is static HTML, paste the JSON-LD block just before the closing head tag. If you use WordPress, install Rank Math or Yoast SEO which have a dedicated structured data section. If you use Wix or Squarespace, add the code in the custom code section of the header. The process takes less than 5 minutes.

Common Schema mistakes and how to validate your code

The most frequent errors are: incorrect or fabricated data, using the generic type instead of a specific one, wrong GPS coordinates, phone number without international format, and duplicating Schema across multiple pages. Validate your code with Google's Rich Results Test and the Schema Markup Validator to make sure everything is correct.

Implementing Schema LocalBusiness on your website is one of the local SEO actions with the best effort-to-result ratio. In less than 10 minutes you can give Google all the information it needs about your business, improve your visibility in search results and get more clicks than your competitors.

Frequently asked questions

What is Schema LocalBusiness in plain terms?
Schema LocalBusiness is invisible code you add to your website to tell Google exactly what your business is, where it is, what hours it keeps and what services it offers. Think of it as a technical data sheet that search engines can read and understand perfectly. Your visitors never see it, but Google, Bing and other search engines do.
Does Schema improve my Google ranking?
Schema is not a direct ranking factor, but it improves your visibility indirectly. Structured data lets Google display rich snippets with your opening hours, location, rating and more. Those results stand out and receive between 20% and 30% more clicks. More clicks mean more traffic and better ranking signals.
Do I need to know how to code to implement Schema?
Not necessarily. There are free online generators, such as the ones from Merkle or TechnicalSEO.com, where you fill in a form with your business details and they produce the JSON-LD code ready to copy and paste into your site. If you use WordPress, plugins such as Rank Math or Yoast SEO also let you add Schema without touching code.
What is the difference between Schema LocalBusiness and Google Business Profile?
Google Business Profile is your listing on Google Maps, visible to every user. Schema LocalBusiness is code inside your website that helps Google understand your business. They complement each other rather than replacing one another. Having both properly configured strengthens your local presence. Google cross-checks the information from the two sources to verify your details.
How do I know whether my website already has Schema LocalBusiness?
You can check with Googles free Rich Results Test. Enter your website address and it will show you which structured data it detects. If no LocalBusiness Schema appears, you need to add it. You can also look at your sites source code and search for LocalBusiness inside script tags of type application/ld+json.
