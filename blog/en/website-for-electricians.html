If you are a self-employed electrician in the Valencia region of Spain, you know that word of mouth is no longer enough. Your potential customers search Google for "electrician in Valencia" or "emergency electrician Alicante" before calling anyone. And if you don't have a professional website for electricians, you are losing those customers to competitors who do. In this guide, we explain exactly what your website needs, how much it costs, and how you can have it ready in less than two weeks.

Why an electrician needs a website in 2026

In 2026, 92% of consumers search for local services online before making a decision. The numbers don't lie: 76% of people who search for a local service on their phone contact a business within the next 24 hours. For a self-employed electrician in Alicante, Elda, Elche or any city in the Valencia region, that translates into direct calls and quote requests without spending a single euro on advertising.

The 6 essential sections of an electrician website

You don't need a 50-page website. An effective electrician website is simple and direct: a homepage that hooks visitors, detailed services (installations, renovations, electrical certificates, EV charging points, 24h emergencies), coverage area, gallery of completed work, customer testimonials, and easy and visible contact with call button and WhatsApp.

Web design for electricians: what really works

Web design for electricians doesn't have to be spectacular. It has to be functional, fast and mobile-optimized. 70% of local service searches are done from phones. Your website should load in under 3 seconds, be responsive, use professional colours and have clear action buttons like "Call me now" or "Get a free quote".

How much does a professional electrician website cost

A custom website costs between 800 and 3,000 euros plus hosting and maintenance. Building it yourself with WordPress costs 100-300 euros per year but requires time. WebAutonomos' rental model offers a professional website from 15 euros per month, including custom design, hosting, domain, maintenance and basic local SEO. No upfront payment and no contract.

How to get started with your electrician website today

Define your main services, set your work area, gather photos of your work, ask clients for testimonials and choose the model that suits you best. A real case: Miguel, a self-employed electrician in Elche, created his professional website and in the first three months received 22 quote requests from the internet. Today, 40% of his new clients come through his website.

Don't wait until everything is perfect to launch your website. It's better to have a simple website published today than a perfect website six months from now. Combine your website with Google Business Profile and start getting clients from Google without spending on advertising.
