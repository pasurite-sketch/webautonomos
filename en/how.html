How it works: your website ready in 24 hours

We build a real demo of your website first, with your own information, and you only pay if you like it. No template to fill in, nothing to configure, and it is all done in English.

This is our "already done" method. Most web offers ask you to commit before you see anything. We do the opposite: you see your finished website first, then decide.

1. You tell us about your business

It takes about two minutes: your trade, your main services, the town where you work and how to reach you (WhatsApp or email). You can use the demo form or simply send us a WhatsApp message.

2. We build your demo in under 24 hours

We write the copy from what you told us and from your Google Business Profile: your services, your opening hours, your real reviews and your photos. We add the WhatsApp button, the contact form, a map, and the legal notice, privacy and cookie pages that Spanish law requires.

3. We review it together

We send you a private link so you can look at it in your own time, and we can go through it together by WhatsApp or video call if you prefer. You tell us what you want changed — texts, photos, colours, sections — and we make the changes within 24 hours. Adjustments before launch are unlimited.

4. You approve it and it goes live

Once you are happy, we connect your domain name, registered in your name, and the website goes live. You pay €15 a month with no lock-in, or a one-off €349. Compare both plans.

After launch

  • One content change per month, done within 24 hours
  • Daily backups
  • 24/7 monitoring and technical maintenance
  • SSL certificate renewed automatically
  • Support by email and WhatsApp
  • Cancel any time, keep your domain

What you don't have to do

Choose a template, learn an editor, compare hosting plans, install plugins, write a Spanish legal notice or a cookie policy, or remember to renew an SSL certificate. That is our job.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to prepare texts or photos?
No. We write the copy from what you tell us and from your Google Business Profile, and we use your photos if you have some. If you don't, we use professional images.
What if I don't like the demo?
Then you don't pay anything. Before launch, adjustments are unlimited: texts, photos, colours, sections. We make each one within 24 hours.
How do changes work after launch?
One content change per month is included: text, photos, opening hours, services, prices or a new section. Send it by WhatsApp or email and it is done within 24 hours.
Do I need any technical knowledge?
None. Hosting, domain, SSL certificate, legal pages, updates and backups are all handled for you.
