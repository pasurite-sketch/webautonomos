What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites store on your device when you visit them. They are used to remember your preferences, improve your browsing experience and collect analytical information about how the website is used.

Types of cookies we use

At webautonomos.es we use the following types of cookies:

  • Technical (necessary) cookies: These enable browsing and the use of basic functions. They do not require consent.
  • Preference cookies: These remember your browsing choices (selected language).
  • Analytics cookies: These help us understand how visitors interact with the site (Google Analytics).
  • Third-party cookies: External services such as Google Analytics may set their own cookies.
4

Specific cookies used

  • _ga (Google Analytics): identifies users. Duration: 2 years.
  • _ga_* (Google Analytics): maintains session state. Duration: 2 years.
  • _gid (Google Analytics): identifies users. Duration: 24 hours.
  • lang (first-party): remembers the selected language. Duration: session.
5

How to manage cookies

You can set your browser to accept, reject or delete cookies:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options > Privacy & Security
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Privacy, search and services
