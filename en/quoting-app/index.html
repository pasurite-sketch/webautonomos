Accurate sums

Quotes with IVA, IRPF withholding and deposit, to the cent

The sums are done by an exact calculation engine, the same on your phone and on the server. The AI never calculates an amount.

  • IVA by rate. Each line has its own VAT rate, and the tax is worked out on the base for each rate, not by adding up rounded amounts line by line.
  • IRPF withholding. If your client is a business and you have to apply it, enter the percentage: Devia works it out on the taxable base and shows the net amount payable.
  • Deposit. As a percentage or a fixed amount, with the balance still to pay.
  • Discounts and sections. Discount per line or on the total, sections and subtotals to organise the job.

What happens when something is missing

  1. It stays blank. A price, a quantity or a VAT rate you haven't given is never filled in with a guess.
  2. It's marked “to confirm”. The line comes out of the totals and the amounts show as provisional.
  3. The quote waits for your check. It can't be set to “Ready to send” while anything is still pending.

And if you import an old quote whose totals don't add up, Devia shows you both figures, the one on the document and the recalculated one, without correcting anything on its own.

FAQ

What people ask us about Devia

How much does Devia cost?

Your first 3 quotes are free. After that you buy a pack: 10 quotes for €12 + VAT, 30 for €29 + VAT or 100 for €79 + VAT. There’s no subscription, and credits never expire.

Is there a free version?

You get 3 free quotes to try all four ways of working, with no card needed. There’s no permanent free plan: after that you only pay for the quotes you make, in packs.

What counts as a quote?

One credit is one finalised quote: it’s used when you set the quote to “Ready to send”, once per quote. Drafts, dictations, imports, edits and duplicates are free and unlimited, and editing or sending a finalised quote later costs nothing more.

Do credits expire?

No. The credits you buy never expire: if you don’t write any quotes for a month, they stay in your account.

Do I get an invoice?

Yes. Every purchase comes with an invoice showing the VAT. When you pay, you can enter your business name and your tax number.

Does the AI set the prices?

No. The AI transcribes what you dictate, reads documents and sorts the lines, but it never makes up a price, a quantity or a VAT rate: anything missing stays “to confirm” and out of the totals. The sums are done by an exact calculation engine, not by the AI.

How do I write a quote on my phone?

With Devia, open the app in your browser or install it on the home screen of your iPhone or Android phone, without going through an app store. Then dictate the quote, photograph a paper quote or type it in. You can finish it later on the computer.

Does Devia work offline?

Yes, for creating and editing quotes: they’re saved on your phone and sync when the connection comes back. Dictation and document reading do need the internet, and Devia tells you so.

What should a quote include?

Your business and client details, a number and a date, how long the offer is valid, the job address, the items with quantities and prices, the base, IVA by rate and the total, and your payment terms. Devia puts them all in the PDF and can add a space for the client’s signature.

Can I write the quote in Spanish for a Spanish client?

Yes. Each quote has its own language: the PDF can come out in English, Spanish or French, whatever language you use the app in. The app itself is available in all three.

Does Devia do invoices?

No. Devia focuses on the quote, from the job to the PDF you send your client. For invoicing, keep using your invoicing software.

Who can see my quotes?

Only your business. Each business only sees its own clients, quotes and photos, and Devia sends nothing to your clients: the quote goes out when you send it.
